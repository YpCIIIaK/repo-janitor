### Not done / open questions

The new security bases are useful follow-up coverage probes, not proof of complete security support. Nua-Bench documents 100 baseline weaknesses across five apps; its 30 high warnings yielded **15 TP / 15 FP**, not 30 independently verified benchmark vulnerabilities. Pikachu yielded **18 TP / 27 FP**. OAuth Labs contributes no high-confidence insecure-code warnings; its protocol-specific weaknesses remain a coverage gap.

Nua-Bench also exposes a Python completeness gap: the regex flags a docstring mentioning yaml.load() at app.py:704, yet excludes the actual unsafe yaml.load(..., Loader=yaml.Loader) call at :731 because it skips any Loader= argument. The docstring warning is FP; the live call is a separate FN retained as a future coverage probe.

The PHP FP cluster needs control-flow and sanitizer handling that respects SQL context and connection charset. A blanket escape() exemption would miss Pikachu's genuine wide-byte injection after character_set_client switches to GBK; escaping alone also leaves unquoted numeric SQL injectable. Those positive controls are retained in the review. Literal HTML examples and sanitized session values are additional cases for future work.

Some explanatory prose, ABI mappings, algorithm diagrams and enum compatibility comments still trigger commented-code; only the proven narrow patterns above were suppressed. iac-docker-socket remains an open precision target. No blanket exclusions for all tests, comments, PHP source or security labs were added.
